GSAP and Lenis retain their upstream licenses in node_modules. No partnership or endorsement is implied by the use of any dependency.

LegalMatterOps naming, trademark availability and domain availability have not been verified. Historic Matter Deadline naming is retained only in research notes.

Paper textures: four original raster surfaces generated for LegalMatterOps with built-in image_gen: pine crumpled paper, sage watercolor cotton, ochre rag paper and terracotta paper. Exact prompts and method are recorded in TEXTURE-PROMPTS.json. PNG originals and optimized WebP files are included.
