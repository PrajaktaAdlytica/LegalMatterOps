A consistent composition.

Typography

Newsreader Variable for editorial headings, figures and the wordmark. Geist Variable for navigation, controls, metadata and body text.

Desktop hero 106px; section headings 56–64px; mobile display 48–55px. Dense workbench typography uses a separate scale.

Spacing & alignment

1280px maximum content width. Desktop gutters 48px, tablet 32px, mobile 22px. Major section spacing 112 / 80 / 65px. Card grids use 24–28px gaps with 22–32px internal padding.

Motion choreography

  1. Hero type, painted-paper fragments, annotations and the readiness card descend from above in a staggered cinematic entrance.
  2. Evidence copy enters from the left; source layers and annotations reveal in sequence.
  3. Desktop scroll pins the five-module rail. Tabs and arrows also navigate the story.
  4. The role map enters from the left; counsel’s explanation arrives from the right.
  5. Scorecards and the audit sheet settle into place.

Interaction language

Live source selectors, role previews, active states, disclosure questions, an inquiry confirmation and a filtered synthetic workbench. Hover changes are subtle: image scale, ink emphasis and a small arrow movement.

Lenis handles smooth wheel scrolling. GSAP ScrollTrigger handles reveals and the pinned chapter. Reduced-motion mode preserves content and uses a native scrolling module rail.

Product truth stays in HTML.

Artwork is illustrative. Legal statuses, source references, role restrictions and prices are real text components. Every demonstration is synthetic; there are no live integrations, client records or conflict clearances.
